Welcome to the National Stock Assessment Enterprise (NSAE) Training Library!

The Library is designed to benefit the NOAA Fisheries stock assessment community by centralizing and archiving the agency's extensive training content. This public platform primarily serves NOAA Fisheries employees, ranging from new hires to experienced staff, by providing structured learning roadmaps and curated technical resources. By organizing materials into guided pathways, the Library will ensure that our personnel can efficiently access and navigate the tools required for onboarding and continuous professional excellence.
